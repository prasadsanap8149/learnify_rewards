# Privacy Policy for Learnify Rewards

**Last updated: August 28, 2025**

## Introduction

Learnify Rewards ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Learnify Rewards mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our educational rewards application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, and profile picture
- **Age Verification**: Date of birth for COPPA compliance and age-appropriate content
- **Educational Progress**: Learning activities completed, quiz scores, and achievement data
- **Reward Points**: LP (Learning Points) balance and transaction history

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Analytics**: App usage patterns, feature interactions, session duration
- **Performance Data**: App crashes, loading times, and technical performance metrics
- **Location Data**: General location (country/region) for content personalization (if enabled)

### Third-Party Information

- **Google Sign-In**: Profile information from Google accounts (with your consent)
- **Social Features**: Information shared when using social features or referrals

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses

- **Educational Services**: Deliver learning content and track progress
- **Reward System**: Manage LP earning, spending, and redemption
- **Account Management**: Create and maintain user accounts
- **Age Verification**: Ensure COPPA compliance and age-appropriate content
- **Customer Support**: Respond to inquiries and provide assistance

### Analytics and Improvement

- **App Analytics**: Understand user behavior to improve our services
- **Performance Monitoring**: Identify and fix technical issues
- **Content Optimization**: Personalize learning experiences
- **Security Monitoring**: Detect and prevent fraudulent activities

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do Not Sell Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

### Limited Sharing

We may share information in these circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: Trusted partners who help operate our services (Firebase, Google Analytics)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Safety**: To protect the safety of our users or prevent illegal activities
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger or acquisition (with user notification)

### Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase**: Authentication, database, analytics, and crashlytics
- **Google Mobile Ads**: Advertisement serving (with age-appropriate content)
- **Google Analytics**: Usage analytics and app performance

## Data Security

### Security Measures

- **Encryption**: All data transmitted is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Secure Storage**: Personal data is stored in secure Firebase databases
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability testing

### Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Learning Progress**: Stored to maintain your educational achievements
- **Analytics Data**: Anonymized after 26 months
- **Deleted Accounts**: Personal data deleted within 30 days of account deletion

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

### Age Verification

- Users must verify their age during registration
- Users under 13 require verifiable parental consent
- Age-appropriate content filtering is applied automatically

### Parental Rights

Parents can:

- Review their child's personal information
- Request deletion of their child's account
- Refuse further collection or use of their child's information
- Contact us at privacy@learnifyrewards.com for assistance

### Limited Data Collection for Children

For users under 13:

- We collect minimal necessary information
- No behavioral advertising
- Enhanced security measures
- Parental notification for any changes

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Management

- **Access**: View your personal information in your account settings
- **Update**: Modify your profile information at any time
- **Delete**: Request account deletion through app settings or email

### Privacy Controls

- **Notifications**: Control push notification preferences
